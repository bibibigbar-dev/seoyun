Shipping Policy

Effective date: March 1, 2026

Processing and dispatch

Paid artwork orders are dispatched within 5 business days after payment is confirmed. This is the time to prepare and hand your order to the carrier, not a promise that it will arrive within 5 business days. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays.

Artwork selection and delivery details

Before paying, confirm the artwork title, its availability, the shipping destination, and delivery charges with support@naseoyun.com. The gallery currently uses a shared payment link for multiple artworks. Missing or incorrect order details may prevent fulfillment; we will contact you promptly to resolve them and agree on any revised dispatch date.

Shipping charges and destinations

Shipping availability and any delivery charges are confirmed before purchase. Any shipping amount charged through checkout will be shown before payment. Do not assume free shipping or worldwide delivery. Contact us for destination-specific options.

Transit time and tracking

Carrier transit time is additional to the processing period and varies by destination and service. Once the order has shipped, we will send available tracking information to the email address associated with your order. Carrier estimates are not guaranteed delivery dates.

Delays

If we cannot dispatch your order within the promised time, we will notify you and seek your agreement to a revised date or provide a refund as required by applicable law. A delay will not be treated as accepted merely because you do not respond.

Packaging and delivery problems

Artwork is packaged for shipment. Please check your delivery address before paying. If the parcel is damaged, missing, or contains the wrong artwork, contact support@naseoyun.com promptly with your order details and, where available, photographs of the item and packaging. Keep the packaging while the issue is reviewed.
