Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 1, 2026

Scope and contact

This Privacy Policy explains the information practices of the Seoyun Art website, artwork inquiries, and purchase-related communications. Contact support@naseoyun.com with privacy questions or requests. Third-party payment services have their own policies.

Information you provide

If you send us an email, we receive the contact details, message, attachments, and order information you include. The on-site inquiry form prepares a draft in your browser. Preparing or copying a draft does not send it to us; we receive it only when you choose to send an email. Inquiry fields are not stored in a website account or order database by the current site code.

Payment information

Payments take place on a Stripe-hosted checkout page. Stripe may collect contact, billing, shipping, payment, device, and transaction information needed to process the payment and prevent fraud. We may receive transaction records, purchaser contact information, and payment status from Stripe to manage orders. This website does not directly collect or store full card numbers or card security codes. See Stripe’s Privacy Policy at Stripe Privacy Policy.

Hosting, fonts, and technical information

The website is hosted on Vercel and loads the Gloock font from Google Fonts. Requests to these services may expose technical information such as IP address, browser details, requested resources, and request times to the service provider. The current site code does not include advertising pixels or analytics trackers, and does not set its own tracking cookies or use local storage for inquiries. Stripe and other third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. See Vercel Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

How information is used

We use information to respond to inquiries, identify the selected artwork, process and fulfill orders, arrange delivery, handle refunds or disputes, maintain transaction records, and comply with applicable obligations. We do not use the current inquiry form to automatically subscribe visitors to marketing messages.

Sharing

Information may be shared with payment, hosting, email, and delivery providers as necessary to provide the relevant service. We may disclose information when required by law or to resolve fraud, security issues, or legal claims. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising through the current website.

Retention and security

We retain communications and order records for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose collected, transaction administration, disputes, and applicable recordkeeping obligations. Different providers may retain their own records under their policies. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure; please avoid including sensitive information that is unnecessary for your inquiry.

Choices and privacy requests

You may contact support@naseoyun.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information we hold about you, or to ask about other rights available under applicable law. We may need to verify your identity and may retain records where legally required. Requests concerning data independently held by Stripe, Google, or Vercel may also need to be directed to that provider.

Children and international processing

The website’s purchasing and inquiry services are intended for adults, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a child’s information has been sent to us, contact us so we can address it. Our providers may process information in the United States or other countries where they operate.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when the website or its information practices change. The applicable date will appear on this page. Questions and requests: support@naseoyun.com.
